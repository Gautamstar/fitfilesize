FitFileSize

Upload limits, handled in the form

When an upload box says something like "20 KB to 50 KB, 200 x 230 pixels" and your file breaks it, you get an offer to fit it right there.

Try it

JPG only, 200 x 230 pixels, size 20 KB to 50 KB

Pick a photo, or use the sample (it is 1200 x 1500 pixels and well over 50 KB). A box appears in the corner of the page; click "Fit it".

How it works

  1. You pick a file in an upload box, as usual.
  2. If the text next to the box states a limit and the file breaks it, a box offers to fit it. Nothing is sent until you click "Fit it".
  3. The fitted file takes the place of yours in the upload box. Submit the form as usual.

Where it checks

From the start, on government, exam and university sites (gov.in, nic.in, ac.in, IBPS, SBI, TCS iON). On any other site, such as a job site, a visa portal or a university abroad, click the FitFileSize button in the toolbar while you are on it and turn on "Check forms on this site". "Also on all other sites" turns it on everywhere at once.

For a drag-and-drop box, or a limit written somewhere else on the page, use "Fit a file by hand" from the same button: type the limit, then download the fitted file.

Your files

A file is sent to fitfilesize.com only when you click "Fit it", and is deleted from the server as soon as the fitted file is back. Privacy
