FitFileSize

When you pick a file in an upload form that states a limit, like "20 KB to 50 KB" or "200 x 230 pixels", and the file breaks it, you get an offer to fit it.

For drag-and-drop boxes, or a limit stated somewhere else on the page.

On government, exam and university sites (gov.in, nic.in, ac.in, IBPS, SBI, TCS iON).

Chrome asks for permission to read pages on every site. It is used only to find the limit next to an upload box.

Your file is sent to fitfilesize.com only when you click "Fit it", and is deleted from the server once it is fitted. Privacy
